Abstract:
 
This report describes the GLOF risk asesment of the 
Samudra Tapu
 glacial lake in 
the Indus River Basin. It contains detailed description about the study area, data 
used, and the methodologies for hydrodynamic GLOF modeling simulations and 
GLOF risk 
asesment for
 various GLOF scenarios. 
High resolution satelite image
s 
are used for maping of 
infrastructure
 and other public utilities along the river 
reach for identifying the elements of exposure
 due to GLOF
. A total of eight GLOF 
scenarios based on the volume of lake 
released
 in case of moraine breach (10%, 
75%, and 50%), weather condition (clear weather/PMP)
, and failure mode 
(overtoping/
piping), were simulated 
using
 the 2D hydrodynamic dam
- break 
modeling
 software
. The results of each simulation and consequent GLOF risk ma
ps 
are also included in this report.
